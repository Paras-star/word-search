## COIN ECONOMY

Fresh player starts with:

* 50 coins

Tutorial:

* +10 → 60

Onboarding:

* 5 words → +10 → 70
* 7 words → +10 → 80
* 9 words → +10 → 90
* 12 words → +10 → 100
* 15 words → +10 → 110

Therefore:

* Tutorial + five onboarding puzzles = **+60 total**
* Fresh player reaches **110 coins when Animals unlocks**

Do not change the 50-coin starting balance.

## ONBOARDING SEQUENCE

Implement:

Step 0:
Tutorial — 5 words

Step 1:
5 words

Step 2:
7 words

Step 3:
9 words

Step 4:
12 words

Step 5:
15 words

Step 6:
Onboarding complete / normal category system unlocked

Each completed onboarding step:

* +10 coins
* exactly once
* no Mystery Dumpling
* no `/reward` route
* no normal +50 reward

## PUZZLE DIFFICULTY

Create an onboarding-only configuration/placement option rather than changing the normal puzzle generator behavior.

Difficulty ramp:

Tutorial:

* very approachable
* primarily horizontal/vertical
* designed to teach selection

5-word:

* primarily horizontal/vertical
* very easy

7-word:

* introduce some diagonal placements

9-word:

* mixed horizontal/vertical/diagonal

12-word:

* mixed directions
* limited reverse placements

15-word:

* closest to normal gameplay
* can use the normal 2–3 reverse-placement behavior

Do NOT modify the normal category generator's existing direction behavior.

The onboarding configuration should be deterministic and use stable seeds/word lists so the onboarding experience does not randomly become significantly harder or impossible.

## PERSISTENCE

Implement the proposed:

`@word-hunt/progress-v2`

Record:

{
coins,
completedLevels,
onboardingStep
}

Where:

* onboardingStep 0 = tutorial not completed
* 1 = tutorial complete, first onboarding puzzle next
* 2 = 5-word puzzle complete
* 3 = 7-word puzzle complete
* 4 = 9-word puzzle complete
* 5 = 12-word puzzle complete
* 6 = 15-word puzzle complete / onboarding finished

Use the existing provider/storage architecture rather than introducing an unrelated persistence system.

The +10 reward and onboardingStep advancement must be committed as one durable operation.

This is important:

A player must never receive the same onboarding +10 reward twice because of:

* double taps
* navigation race
* app restart
* browser refresh
* Android app close/reopen
* replaying a completed onboarding puzzle
* returning to an earlier route
* pressing back and re-entering the puzzle

If persistence fails:

* do not advance the player as if the reward succeeded
* do not allow the reward to be silently lost
* make the operation safely retryable

## MIGRATION

* Read the existing `@word-hunt/completed-levels`

* preserve it
* mark onboarding as complete
* do NOT award onboarding coins
* do NOT force the player through the tutorial

Only a genuinely fresh player with no existing saved progress should receive:

* 50 starting coins
* tutorial
* onboarding sequence

Keep the old keys readable for migration.

Do not modify the dumpling storage key.
