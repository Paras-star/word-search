## PHASE 2 TARGET FLOW

A completely fresh player should experience:

### Step 0 — New player

* 50 coins

### Step 1 — Tutorial Puzzle

A dedicated tutorial puzzle before the normal category system.

Tutorial:

* Exactly 5 words
* Designed to teach the player how Word Hunt works
* Should use the existing puzzle engine
* Should be easier than normal puzzles
* Prefer mostly horizontal/vertical placements
* Avoid making it frustrating
* The tutorial should clearly communicate what the player needs to do
* Completion reward: **+10 coins**
* NO Mystery Dumpling reward
* Tutorial completion must persist
* Player must NOT be able to replay the tutorial to farm coins

After completion:

* Coins: 60

## IMPORTANT PROGRESSION RULE

The onboarding sequence is a one-time progression.

A player who completes:
Tutorial → 5 → 7 → 9 → 12 → 15

must never receive those onboarding coin rewards again.

Closing/reopening the app, restarting it, rescanning the Expo QR, or refreshing the browser must preserve the onboarding state.

Do NOT rely on in-memory state only.

Use the existing persistence architecture where possible.
