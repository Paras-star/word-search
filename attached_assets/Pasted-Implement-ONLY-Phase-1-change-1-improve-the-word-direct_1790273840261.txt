Implement ONLY Phase 1 change #1: improve the word-direction distribution in the existing puzzle generator.

Relevant implementation is in `game/puzzle.ts`, especially `DIRECTIONS`, candidate collection, and `generatePuzzle`.

Current behavior:

* All 8 straight-line directions are supported.
* For each word, every legal start/direction candidate is collected and one is randomly selected.
* Reverse directions are currently unrestricted.
* The generator uses seeded randomness and must remain deterministic for the same seed.

Goal:
Make puzzles easier to visually search without removing the challenge.

Treat these as reverse directions:

* left / right-to-left
* up / bottom-to-top
* down-left
* up-left
* i.e. directions whose natural reading progression is reversed relative to left-to-right/top-to-bottom.

Do NOT remove any of the 8 supported directions.

Implementation requirements:

1. Prefer approximately 2–3 reverse-direction word placements per puzzle.
2. Do NOT impose a rigid cap that can make generation fail.
3. When the preferred distribution cannot be satisfied because of grid geometry or existing letter overlaps, gracefully fall back to any legal candidate.
4. Preserve the existing seeded/randomized behavior.
5. Preserve word overlap behavior.
6. Preserve the existing maximum grid sizes and category data.
7. Do not change the selection/gesture system.
8. Do not change the UI.
9. Do not change scoring, hints, rewards, persistence, or progression.
10. Do not modify package.json or dependencies.

Important:
The direction preference must be applied during candidate selection/placement, not by changing the `DIRECTIONS` array in a way that removes directions.

After implementation, add a lightweight validation script or temporary validation approach if appropriate that generates many seeded puzzles and reports:

* total words
* number of reverse-direction placements
* whether every listed word is still placed
* whether any puzzle generation fails

Test enough different seeds/categories to demonstrate that the generator remains solvable and that reverse directions generally stay around the intended 2–3 range.
