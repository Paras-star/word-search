`Daily Puzzle → Back → Calendar → tap another date → noticeable delay → Daily Puzzle`

Please inspect why the Daily route is taking time to open.

* unnecessary route remounting
* repeated puzzle generation

`Calendar → Date A → Back → Calendar → Date B → Puzzle`
