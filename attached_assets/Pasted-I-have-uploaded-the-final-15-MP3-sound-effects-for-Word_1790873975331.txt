Please connect them to these exact gameplay events:

### 1. letter-tap.mp3
Play a very short tap sound whenever the player successfully selects/adds a letter while dragging across the puzzle grid.

Important:
- It should trigger during active letter selection.
- Do not play it repeatedly for the same letter if the selection logic fires duplicate events.
- Do not play it when the player simply touches/releases without selecting a new letter.

### 2. word-found.mp3
Play when the player successfully finds a normal puzzle word.

Trigger exactly once when the valid word is confirmed.

### 3. bonus-word.mp3
Play when the player successfully discovers a bonus word.

Trigger exactly once when the bonus word is confirmed.

Do not play both word-found and bonus-word for the same bonus-word discovery.

### 4. invalid-selection.mp3
Play when the player releases an invalid word selection.

Trigger once per invalid release.

Do not play it for harmless touches or cancellations where no actual word selection was attempted.

### 5. hint-used.mp3
Play when a hint is successfully consumed/used.

Trigger exactly once per actual hint use.

Do not play it merely because the hint UI is opened.

### 6. button-click.mp3
Use for appropriate ordinary interactive buttons throughout the game.

Examples include:
- Play/start buttons
- Category selection
- Mode selection
- Back buttons
- Other normal UI buttons

Do NOT use button-click for events that already have a dedicated sound such as:
- finding a word
- using a hint
- receiving coins
- completing a level
- opening/revealing a dumpling

Avoid excessive or duplicate button sounds.

### 7. coin-reward.mp3
Play when coins are actually awarded to the player.

Trigger once when the coin reward is granted.

Do not play simply because a screen displaying the current coin balance is opened.

### 8. level-complete.mp3
Play when a normal puzzle is successfully completed.

Trigger once at the actual completion event.

Do not play repeatedly if the results screen re-renders.

### 9. dumpling-opening.mp3
Play at the actual beginning of the Mystery Dumpling opening animation/sequence, when the basket/container begins opening.

It should happen before the reveal sound.

### 10. dumpling-reveal.mp3
Play at the moment the Mystery Dumpling is revealed.

Do not trigger it when the opening sequence merely starts.

### 11. common-rarity.mp3
Play when the revealed dumpling is confirmed/displayed as Common rarity.

### 12. uncommon-rarity.mp3
Play when the revealed dumpling is confirmed/displayed as Uncommon rarity.

### 13. rare-rarity.mp3
Play when the revealed dumpling is confirmed/displayed as Rare rarity.

### 14. epic-rarity.mp3
Play when the revealed dumpling is confirmed/displayed as Epic rarity.

### 15. legendary-rarity.mp3
Play when the revealed dumpling is confirmed/displayed as Legendary rarity.

The rarity sound must correspond to the actual rarity received. Only ONE rarity sound should play for each dumpling reveal.
