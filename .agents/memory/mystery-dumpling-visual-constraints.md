Use only the supplied 30 dumpling artworks, exact catalog names and five supplied rarity tiers. The individually supplied PNGs for every rarity supersede all old combined sprite sheets. Keep the supplied Collection Room as one combined environment; never split it into separate rooms or replace it.

**Why:** The user explicitly defined the supplied artwork, catalog, and combined room as authoritative and prohibited generated, redrawn, recolored, renamed, or substituted collectibles.

**How to apply:** Any future Mystery Dumpling feature must reference or crop the supplied individual assets without altering their designs, and must exclude embedded name labels. Effects may surround a dumpling but must not modify the artwork itself.
