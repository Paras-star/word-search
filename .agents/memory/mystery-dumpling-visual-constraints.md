---
name: Mystery Dumpling visual constraints
description: Non-negotiable visual-source rules for future Mystery Dumpling work.
---

Use only the supplied 30 dumpling artworks, exact catalog names and five supplied rarity tiers. The individually supplied Common and Uncommon PNGs supersede their old combined sprite sheets. Keep the supplied Collection Room as one combined environment; never split it into separate rooms or replace it.

**Why:** The user explicitly defined the supplied artwork, catalog, and combined room as authoritative and prohibited generated, redrawn, recolored, renamed, or substituted collectibles.

**How to apply:** Any future Mystery Dumpling feature must reference or crop the supplied assets without altering their designs. For Common and Uncommon, use the individual source PNGs and exclude their embedded name labels. Effects may surround a dumpling but must not modify the artwork itself.
